#### [RAG](https://github.com/CaydenZheng/RAG)

Make local knowledge searchable. Give agents tools.

A local knowledge retrieval and question-answering workflow, combining **dense retrieval and BM25** with RRF fusion. **Tool calling and MCP** connect information retrieval with actions.

<samp>FastAPI · PocketFlow · Dense+BM25 · RRF · MCP</samp>

#### [MindTrace · 日迹](https://github.com/CaydenZheng/mindtrace-web)

You write. AI holds up a mirror.

An application for **everyday journaling and reflection**, exploring how AI can help people revisit their thoughts and use their records to better understand themselves.

<samp>Next.js 14 · FastAPI · SQLite · DeepSeek</samp>

#### [SASRec](https://github.com/CaydenZheng/sasrec)

A reproduction of **self-attentive sequential recommendation**, using MovieLens-1M to study sequence modeling and recommendation evaluation.

<samp>PyTorch · ml-1m · NDCG@10 0.59 / HR@10 0.82</samp>
